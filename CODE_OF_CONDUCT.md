# Community Code of Conduct

We aim for a safe, respectful, inclusive and evidence-driven community.

## Expected
- Respect people across backgrounds, skills, identities and locations.
- Communicate constructively and professionally.
- Welcome questions and beginner contributions.
- Credit others accurately.
- Disagree with ideas without personal attacks.
- Protect privacy and follow task scope.

## Unacceptable
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam, deceptive claims, impersonation, unauthorized fundraising, coercion, sharing private information or knowingly misrepresenting evidence/results.

Maintainers may edit, close, reject or remove contributions and restrict participation to protect the community. If you experience a concern, contact a maintainer privately through GitHub; do not publish sensitive details.
