What we are—and are not—claiming

A computed hash can help compare file bytes. By itself, it does not establish authorship, source authenticity, time, truth, safety, or preservation of context. These are experiments, not production certification.

Use synthetic/public materials only. Do not upload sensitive evidence, personal data, secrets, or patent-sensitive work to this community repository.

Curious? Inspect, question, improve.

Contributions are welcome through written, public GitHub issues and pull requests. Clear reasoning and honest limitations matter more than confident claims.
