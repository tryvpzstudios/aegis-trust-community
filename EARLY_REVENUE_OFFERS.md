# AEGIS TRUST — Early Revenue Offers (Pilot Hypotheses)

**Status: PROPOSED — not yet sold, validated, or delivered.**

Revenue-first means testing paid demand early without misrepresenting prototype capabilities. These offers are research/consulting pilots, not forensic certification or truth/authenticity determinations.

## Offer 1 — Digital Evidence Workflow Readiness Review
**Buyer hypothesis:** small media teams, NGOs, trust-and-safety teams, legal-support operations, incident response teams, and organizations handling user-submitted media.

**Deliverable:** a scoped workflow map, risk/limitation register, provenance and integrity control checklist, data-retention/privacy recommendations, and prioritized improvement plan.

**Boundaries:** review is based on client-described workflows and public/synthetic materials unless a separate approved agreement and secure handling plan exists. No legal advice, forensic certification, or conclusions about the truth/authenticity of specific content.

**Pilot validation:** conduct discovery interviews; ask about current pain, budget owner, urgency, existing workflow, and willingness to pay. Do not treat interest as purchase intent.

## Offer 2 — Synthetic Provenance Workflow Demonstrator
**Buyer hypothesis:** product teams evaluating evidence/provenance workflows.

**Deliverable:** a bounded prototype using synthetic data showing intake metadata, integrity-check records, provenance relationships, conservative status labels, limitations, and a report export.

**Boundaries:** not production-ready, no private evidence, no identity/authenticity guarantee, no public deployment without a separate security review.

**Acceptance criteria:** agreed use-case, synthetic test fixtures, reproducible demonstration, documented limitations, and handover.

## Offer 3 — Provenance Standards & Integration Brief
**Buyer hypothesis:** teams considering provenance standards or evidence workflow integrations.

**Deliverable:** a short, scoped technical research brief comparing selected public standards and candidate integration approaches, with assumptions, trade-offs, gaps, and next-step prototype recommendation.

**Boundaries:** scope and sources must be agreed; no claim of complete standards coverage or compliance certification.

## Commercial validation gates
1. Identify a narrow buyer/problem segment.
2. Conduct written discovery (user preference: no phone calls).
3. Record evidence of problem urgency and existing spending/workarounds.
4. Offer a clearly scoped pilot with written deliverables, price, timeline, privacy/IP terms, and exclusions.
5. Obtain explicit agreement before any client work.
6. Deliver and collect feedback; only then evaluate repeatability and productization.
