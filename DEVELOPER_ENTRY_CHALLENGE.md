# Developer Entry Challenge — AEGIS TRUST

## Purpose
A small, public, synthetic-only challenge to help new contributors understand our evidence-first approach and demonstrate useful work without needing access to private systems.

## Challenge
Review the synthetic evidence graph fixtures and answer the following:

1. What can the graph support based strictly on the represented relations?
2. What remains **INDETERMINATE**, **DISPUTED**, or **NOT_CHECKED**, and why?
3. Identify at least three ways a careless system could overstate the conclusion.
4. Propose one additional synthetic test fixture that exercises an edge case.
5. Provide a short reproducible validation plan.

## Required boundaries
- Use only public repository files and synthetic data.
- Do not infer real-world truth, identity, intent, authenticity, or safety from this exercise.
- Do not introduce personal data, private evidence, secrets, or external datasets.
- No access to private core code or systems is required or offered.

## Submission format
Open an issue titled **[ENTRY CHALLENGE] Your handle** and include:
- Repository files reviewed
- Your reasoning (clearly separate observations from assumptions)
- Proposed test fixture (JSON or a description)
- Validation steps/results
- Limitations and possible false-positive/false-negative risks
- Optional: link to a public sample of relevant work

This is an introductory public exercise, not an employment test. It does not promise employment, compensation, prizes, equity, or acceptance. Any paid or IP-sensitive work requires a separate written agreement before work begins.

## Maintainer review rubric
- Evidence and claim separation
- Correct handling of uncertainty and conflicts
- Reproducibility
- Clear limitations
- Safety/privacy discipline
- Useful, focused contribution

## Start
Read `README.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `SECURITY.md`, and `TASKS.md` first. Wait for maintainer acknowledgement before treating a proposed task as assigned.
