# Public Scope & Intellectual Property Protection

This repository is a **public collaboration surface**, not a disclosure of AEGIS's confidential core, full business strategy, or proprietary implementation.

## Public contributors may work on
- Public-facing documentation, accessibility, localization and onboarding
- Synthetic datasets and reproducible public benchmarks
- Generic interoperability research using public sources
- Isolated, non-sensitive demo components explicitly approved by maintainers
- User-facing mockups and feedback that do not expose confidential workflows

## Never publish or request
- Confidential core architecture, proprietary algorithms, internal system designs, private roadmap details or security-sensitive implementation
- Secrets, credentials, signing keys, deployment configuration or private infrastructure
- Real customer/user evidence, personal data or restricted datasets
- Patent-sensitive inventions, unpublished technical mechanisms or invention disclosures
- Private repository code, internal financial/legal records, customer lists, negotiations or partner information

## Disclosure and review
- Maintainers must review all public material before publication.
- Public tasks are intentionally limited; a task description is not permission to disclose or reverse-engineer private internals.
- When uncertain, stop and ask privately before posting.
- Publicly describe only what is necessary to explain a contribution; keep implementation details and novel mechanisms in the private workspace.
- Do not submit confidential information in issues, PRs, discussions, or public benchmarks.
- Public contributions do not gain access to private systems.

## IP and contributor terms
Before any work that may involve confidential, patent-sensitive, paid, or commercially integrated scope, the parties must agree in writing on confidentiality, scope, compensation (if any), attribution, and IP ownership/licensing. Do not begin such work before agreement.

## Release rule
**No public disclosure of a core invention or sensitive technical mechanism without an explicit maintainer decision and IP review.** Public releases should be safe, deliberate, and limited to what the project chooses to disclose.

This policy does not guarantee legal protection or establish a patent. Seek qualified counsel for IP decisions.
