# AEGIS TRUST — Open Trust Challenge #001

## Can you assess a digital claim without pretending certainty?

### Scenario (fully synthetic)
A social post says: "This photograph proves that a factory released toxic material into a nearby river yesterday."

The post includes:
- One image with no original file available
- A caption with no named source
- A location claim, but no coordinates
- No timestamp metadata available
- Two commenters disagree about where the image was taken

### Your challenge
Using only this synthetic scenario, propose an evidence-first assessment workflow.

1. What claims must be separated and assessed independently?
2. What evidence would be needed to verify the image's origin, time, and location?
3. What observations are useful but insufficient to establish the claim?
4. How should uncertainty and conflicting evidence be represented?
5. What wording would avoid falsely labelling the post true or false?
6. Design a minimal evidence record with source, collection time, integrity, limitations, and status fields.

### Submission
Reply in this issue or submit a public Markdown proposal. Keep it concise, reproducible, and evidence-focused.

### Ground rules
- This is synthetic; do not investigate real people or organizations.
- Do not make accusations or contact anyone.
- Missing evidence is not proof of fabrication.
- A signal or mismatch is not, by itself, proof of deception.
- Clearly separate facts, hypotheses, and unknowns.

### What we're looking for
Careful reasoning, transparent limitations, practical methods, and ideas that improve digital trust.
