## Short invitation
AEGIS TRUST is building and testing open experiments around digital evidence, provenance, media integrity and responsible trust systems.

We have a public synthetic-only developer entry challenge for contributors interested in evidence reasoning, test design, privacy, accessibility, documentation and interoperability.

Start here:
https://github.com/tryvpzstudios/aegis-trust-community

We welcome focused, reproducible contributions. Please read the contributor guidelines and claim a task before starting. No employment, compensation, prizes or equity are promised.

## Technical-community version
We're inviting independent developers and researchers to review a small open prototype exploring conservative evidence-graph reasoning.

The challenge uses synthetic data only and asks contributors to:
- distinguish observations from claims
- preserve uncertainty and contradictions
- identify overclaiming risks
- propose a reproducible edge-case test

Repository: https://github.com/tryvpzstudios/aegis-trust-community

No private systems or sensitive data are involved. Please review CONTRIBUTING.md before proposing work.
